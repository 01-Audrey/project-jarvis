# 03 - Email

## Goal
Let Jarvis read and summarize email, then (later) draft replies.

## Plan
- Use a **separate test inbox**, not my main email
- **Read-only first**: no sending until I trust the setup
- Require my approval before any outgoing message

## Why the caution
An email can contain hidden instructions meant to trick an AI agent
(prompt injection). Starting read-only and with a throwaway inbox limits the damage.

## Also note
On free Gemini tiers, content may be used to improve Google products, so
no sensitive email until I move to a paid key or a local model.
